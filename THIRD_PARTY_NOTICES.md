# Third-party sources

The HSD source under `external/hsd/` comes from
https://github.com/ZhouYuxuanYX/Hierarchical-Speculative-Decoding at commit
`b8575e2ad42bcf2b32d0c6f3481eb49099453f33` (Apache-2.0).
Its LICENSE and upstream copyright headers are retained.
The inference runner applies local HSD evaluation adaptations to an isolated Transformers runtime.

Model and GSM8K source identifiers and revisions are recorded in
`configs/protocol.json`. Their respective upstream licenses apply.
